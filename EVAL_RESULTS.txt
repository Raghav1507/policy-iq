Question: Q7. I closed my account yesterday. When will I get my money?
As of:    2026-08-15
Status:   CONFLICT_RESOLVED
Answer:   A merchant may close the account with 30 days' written notice (clause 5.1). Pending settlements are released within 10 working days of closure, after deducting outstanding dues (clause 5.2). So if the account closed yesterday (assuming the 30-day notice was served), the money is released within 10 working days of the closure date. Note: the FAQ says 'within 10 days'; the master policy says 10 working days, which is authoritative.
Resolved: FAQ-5 loosely says '10 days'; master clause 5.2 specifies 10 working days. Master policy governs.
