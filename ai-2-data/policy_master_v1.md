# Merchant Services Policy — Master Document
Version 1.0 — Effective 01 January 2026

## 1. Settlement
1.1 Settlement for UPI transactions is T+1 (next working day). Working days exclude Sundays and bank holidays.
1.2 Card transactions settle T+2.
1.3 If a settlement date falls on a bank holiday, settlement occurs on the next working day.

## 2. MDR (Merchant Discount Rate)
2.1 UPI MDR for merchants with monthly volume up to ₹10 lakh is 0.30% of the transaction value.
2.2 UPI MDR for merchants with monthly volume above ₹10 lakh is 0.50% of the transaction value, subject to the cap in 2.3.
2.3 MDR on any single UPI transaction shall not exceed ₹15.
2.4 GST at 18% is charged on the MDR amount and is not part of the MDR cap.

## 3. Refunds and Chargebacks
3.1 Merchants may initiate a refund within 90 days of the original transaction.
3.2 MDR charged on a refunded transaction is not returned to the merchant, except as provided in 3.3.
3.3 If a refund is initiated within 24 hours of the transaction, the MDR is reversed in full.
3.4 Chargeback disputes must be raised by the customer's bank within 120 days.

## 4. Limits
4.1 Per-transaction UPI limit is ₹1,00,000.
4.2 Merchants in category "Capital Markets" and "Insurance" may accept up to ₹2,00,000 per transaction, see 4.4.
4.3 Daily limit per customer account is ₹1,00,000 across all UPI transactions.
4.4 The enhanced limit in 4.2 applies only to merchants who have completed enhanced KYC (Annexure C).

## 5. Account Closure
5.1 A merchant may close the account with 30 days written notice.
5.2 Pending settlements are released within 10 working days of closure, after deducting outstanding dues.
