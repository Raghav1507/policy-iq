# Circular MS/2026/17 — Amendments to Merchant Services Policy
Issued 20 July 2026 — Effective 01 August 2026 unless stated otherwise

Pursuant to regulatory guidance, the following clauses of the Master Document v1.0 are amended:

A. Clause 1.1 is replaced: UPI settlement is T+0 (same day, by 11:59 PM) for merchants with an active "Instant Settlement" add-on, and T+1 for all others.

B. Clause 2.1 is replaced: UPI MDR for merchants with monthly volume up to ₹10 lakh is 0.25%.

C. Clause 2.3 is replaced: the MDR cap of ₹15 applies only to merchants with monthly volume up to ₹10 lakh. For merchants above ₹10 lakh, the cap is ₹25.

D. Clause 3.3 is deleted. Clause 3.2 now applies without exception.
   (Effective 01 September 2026.)

E. Clause 4.3 is amended: the daily limit per customer account is ₹1,00,000 or the limit set by the customer's own bank, whichever is lower.

F. All other clauses remain unchanged.
