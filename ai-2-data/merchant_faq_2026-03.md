# Merchant FAQ (last updated 12 March 2026)

**When will I get my UPI money?**
Next working day. Card money comes in two working days.

**What do I pay on a UPI transaction?**
0.30% for small merchants, 0.50% for large ones. There is no upper limit on the fee, so very large transactions cost more.

**If a customer asks for a refund the same day, do I get my fee back?**
Yes, if you refund within 24 hours we reverse the fee.

**What is the maximum amount a customer can pay at my shop?**
₹1,00,000 per transaction. Insurance and capital markets merchants can accept ₹2,00,000.

**Can I close my account any time?**
Yes, with 30 days' notice. Pending money is released within 10 days.
